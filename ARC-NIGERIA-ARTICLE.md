# ⚡ Afritop — Buy Airtime, Data & Electricity in Africa, Paid in USDC

**A story of building a stablecoin-powered top-up platform for Africa — by @siribraheem33 (Afritop: @AfriTopapp)**

---

## What is Afritop?

Afritop is a web app that lets you buy **airtime**, **data bundles** and **prepaid electricity** for Africa — and pay with **USDC straight from your crypto wallet**.

**No bank card. No account. No sign-up. Credits land in seconds.**

It's the simplest way to explain it: *"Pay for your top-up like you pay for gas on-chain — wallet opens, you approve, it's done."*

---

## The problem I set out to solve

Let's be honest about life in Africa:

1. **Bank cards are still a struggle.** Many people — especially diaspora sending money home — either don't have usable international cards, or their cards get blocked by fraud filters the moment a charge comes from another country.
2. **Top-up apps are scattered.** You need one app for MTN, another for Glo, another for your electricity DisCo. Different logins, different payment methods.
3. **Cards leak your details.** Every top-up site you use stores your card number. Every breach is a risk.

I wanted to build the thing I wished existed: **a single place where you can top up any network in Africa, and pay with money you already hold in your wallet — USDC.**

---

## How Afritop works (simple, 4 steps)

1. **Pick a country** — Nigeria 🇳🇬, Ghana 🇬🇭, Kenya 🇰🇪 or South Africa 🇿🇦
2. **Pick a service** — Airtime, Data bundle, or Prepaid electricity — choose your network and the amount
3. **Pay with USDC** — your wallet pops up instantly, you approve the exact amount (no redirects, no delays)
4. **Done** — we verify the payment on-chain, deliver the top-up, and show you a receipt

That's it. You don't even need to create an account — connect your wallet and your full order history follows you on the cloud.

## Why USDC? Why on-chain? (the part the ARC community will love)

- **USDC is the stablecoin people already trust** — 1 USDC = 1 USD, no price swings, no surprises.
- **Payments happen on Arc** — Circle's EVM Layer-1 built specifically for stablecoin payments. Fast, cheap, and gas can be paid in USDC itself.
- **On-chain verification** — we don't just take your word (or your screenshot). The platform reads the transaction **on-chain**, confirms the recipient, amount and sender against public RPCs, and only then delivers the top-up. **No blind trust.**
- **Self-custody** — you pay directly from your own wallet. Afritop never touches or stores your private keys. Your money stays yours until the moment you choose to spend it.
- **Receipts with proof** — every order links back to its transaction on the block explorer. Your history is provable, not just a text message.

This is what "crypto payments for everyday life" actually looks like — not trading, not speculation. **Real utility.**
